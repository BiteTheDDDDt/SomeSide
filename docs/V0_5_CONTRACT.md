# SomeSide 0.5 integration contract

- Catalogs: 24 passive relics, 8 weapons, 8 active equipment. `Content.rarity_name/color/rank` share common/uncommon/rare/legendary identity across world loot, HUD and inventory. `color` is rarity; `theme_color` preserves the effect palette.
- Existing starters remain ranger pulse_rifle + grenade and vanguard arc_blade + shockwave. Random rewards exclude all four starter IDs, all teammates' worn slots, ground gear and unopened facility gear. `state.loot_history` stores up to eight recent gear IDs and four recent passive IDs.
- `_random_loot(source)` uses ambient/cache/trial/boss; ordinary category weights target 88% passive, 7% weapon, 5% active. `rarity_weights(source)` improves later stages and boss/trial outcomes. `_random_equipment(source, excluded)` samples active equipment only. Recent history is soft: if it empties a category, choose its least-recent hard-available candidate; a sole immediately repeated ordinary candidate falls back to passive. Never relax hard exclusions. Three-choice groups are unique, with at least two passives and at most one gear option.
- New passives: plating, magnet, harvest, battery, frost, momentum, toxin, echo, piercer, resonator, phoenix, nova. New weapons: flamethrower, boomerang, storm_staff, sun_lance. New actives: graviton, turret, meteor, time_warp. Each has a real rule effect tested in `test_content.gd`; catalog presence alone does not count as behavior validation.
- `state.deployables` contains authoritative turrets (cap 4); `state.effects` contains delayed meteor impacts (cap 24). Timed buffs and enemy status fields replicate through full snapshots. Renderer consumes them without modifying state. Additional piercing is capped at three; lance totals nine, boomerang seven each outbound and return. Damage/proc depth and event budgets stay bounded.
- UI keeps held passive icons visible in a 12-column, at most two-row strip. `_hovered_loadout` returns the actual hovered owned item/slot; full description requires no Alt and `_get_command` suppresses E while inspecting. Ground target selection still uses exact kind/ID and F cycling. Tab defaults to owned gear plus held passives; `_inventory_filters` and `_populate_inventory(filter_key)` expose owned/passive/weapon/equipment pages in one scroll view.
- `--smoke-advanced` only affects automated host/client runs. Four peers receive the four new weapons and active abilities; reports include `advanced` and `observed` (deployables, effects, chrono, projectile_kinds). Tool `-Advanced` requires each peer to observe the new replicated mechanics. Normal gameplay remains unchanged.
- Release: `dist/v0.5.0/SomeSide.exe`, preserving previous versions. Final validation combines all tests, four source peers, four exported peers with advanced loadouts, and two exported peers through delayed/lossy UDP with reliable results delivery.
