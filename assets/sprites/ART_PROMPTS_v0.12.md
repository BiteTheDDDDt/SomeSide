# SomeSide v0.12 animation artwork

## Layout correction prompts (final canyon and boss sheets)

The first canyon and boss attempts had neighboring silhouettes too close for clean rectangular crops. These final built-in imagegen edits were saved over the new v2 files before integration. Both now use paired rows of four frames per actor; `actors.json` contains explicit crops, not a uniform-grid assumption.

### canyon-enemies-v2

```text
Use case: identity-preserve. Correct the supplied animation sprite sheet layout for actual game use. Keep exactly the same three creature identities, palettes, 8 distinct animation poses each and crisp pixel-art style, but re-lay out into exactly SIX ROWS and FOUR COLUMNS on a 1024x1536 transparent image. Every sprite occupies at most 65% of its cell width and height, with AT LEAST 35 fully transparent pixels of padding on ALL FOUR sides of EVERY sprite. CRITICAL: no body, tail, limb or horn may touch any neighboring sprite; no sprite may cross its cell boundary. No background, shading behind sprites, glow, shadow, text, labels or visible grid. Rows1+2: eight poses of rust-orange horned armored charger. Rows3+4: eight poses of tan spade-clawed sand burrower. Rows5+6: eight poses of silver/navy cyan crystal-winged drone. Read left to right across paired rows: six coherent full locomotion-cycle poses, then attack anticipation, then attack release. Bodies entirely inside each roomy cell. Consistent scale for each creature, core/head registration identical in each cell. Do NOT add creatures or change colors or scale among frames. Full actual transparent RGBA background, straight clean pixel edges. Correct generous separated cell layout is the highest priority.
```

### bosses-v2

```text
Use case: identity-preserve. Correct the supplied 24-frame boss sprite atlas layout. Keep the exact same three original boss identities, anatomy, palettes and crisp fine pixel-art appearance, but re-layout on a portrait 1536x2048 transparent atlas in exactly SIX ROWS and FOUR COLUMNS. Each sprite is SMALLER INSIDE its roomy cell: body including fully extended arms, crown, halo, crystals and feet must occupy at most 65% of cell width and 70% of cell height, leaving AT LEAST 50 fully transparent pixels on every side. Especially the stone boss's extended fist MUST stay inside its own cell and MUST NOT touch or overlap the next boss. No backdrop, glow behind sprites, shadow, floor, grid, text or numbers. Rows1+2: Spore Matriarch, moss/chitin/fungal crown/orange sacs; first4idle slowbreathing, next2anticipation brace, final2release/recovery. Rows3+4: stone quadruped Colossus with slate/bronze and amber fissures; first4idle limbshift, next2brace, final2punch and recovery. Rows5+6: purple ivory floating Prism Regent with eye halo and crystals; first4hover armshift, next2gatherenergy withhands, final2spreadarms and recover. Do not include VFX/projectiles. Preserve exact scale and central body anchor across all eight poses of each boss. Distinct successive coherent motion frames, precise hard pixel clusters. Entire silhouettes safely enclosed by transparent gutters. Separate all 24 frames; the roomy registered cells are essential for rectangular game cropping.
```


Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, 2026-10-05. The six versioned PNGs are unmodified generated RGBA outputs with their original alpha. Original v1 sheets remain in Git. Crops, registration anchors and animation timing are authored in `actors.json`; weapons and relics remain separate layers. No interpolated or duplicated source frames are used to inflate the frame count.

## ranger-v2

Saved asset: `ranger-v2.png`. Identity reference: `heroes-v1.png`.

```text
Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: production 16-frame animation sprite sheet for SomeSide. The supplied image is a character identity reference only; expand ONLY Ranger (the TOP ROW figure): lean explorer, ivory and desaturated teal armor, cyan face visor, compact backpack, short rust-orange scarf. Create exactly FOUR ROWS and FOUR COLUMNS, sixteen isolated full-body animation sprites, equal cells on a 1024x1024 square canvas, alpha transparent background. No other character. Keep the exact helmet, armor, scarf, backpack, colors and proportions of this reference character. Crisp fine 16-bit pixel art, deliberate hard pixel clusters, no smooth rendering, no blur. All sprites face RIGHT in strict side view and keep the exact same body scale. Every helmet center and shoulder pivot must occupy the same relative position inside its cell across all 16 frames, no torso rotation, no accidental shrinking or stretching. Feet align to identical ground line for grounded poses. Generous empty transparent padding, no clipping. No gun, sword, held object, trails, ground shadow, backdrop, text, labels, grid, border or decoration. Arms close to body for separately overlaid aiming weapon.
Row 1: four very subtle idle breathing frames; same planted feet, stable chest and helmet, scarf tip changes gently.
Row 2 + Row 3 are one continuous eight-frame run cycle, read left to right: right foot front contact, compression/recoil, left leg passing, upward recovery, left foot front contact, compression/recoil on other side, right leg passing, upward recovery to first contact. Actually change leg positions in each consecutive frame; the two passing poses must bridge opposite contacts, not repeat poses. Keep the helmet and torso height consistent to prevent animation jitter; leg articulation and scarf convey motion.
Row 4 left to right: rising jump with knees tucked; falling with legs extending downward to prepare landing; forward dash with tucked feet and scarf stretched back, steady upright torso; landing compression with bent knees and BOTH feet on ground, chest only slightly lower. These are bodies used in a small 2D game, precise registered animation alignment is essential. Make ONLY the transparent sprite sheet.
```
